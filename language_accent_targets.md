# Target Languages & Accent/Dialect Coverage

30 languages, each with regional accent targets for maximum diversity in deepfake detection training data.

## 1. English (`en`)
- **American:** General American, Southern, NYC, Midwest, AAVE, Chicano
- **British:** RP, Cockney, Scouse, Scottish, Welsh, Northern
- **Australian:** General, Broad, cultivated
- **Indian:** North Indian, South Indian, Bengali-accented
- **Nigerian:** Lagos English, Igbo-accented, Yoruba-accented
- **South African:** Johannesburg, Cape Town, Afrikaans-accented
- **Singaporean:** Singlish-influenced
- **Irish:** Dublin, Cork, rural

## 2. Mandarin Chinese (`zh-cn`)
- **Beijing:** standard Putonghua, heavy Beijing r-coloring
- **Sichuan-accented:** Southwestern Mandarin influence
- **Cantonese-accented:** Guangdong speakers' Mandarin
- **Taiwanese Mandarin:** softer tones, vocabulary differences
- **Northeastern (Dongbei):** distinctive intonation
- **Shanghainese-accented:** Wu influence on Mandarin

## 3. Hindi (`hi`)
- **Delhi/Khariboli:** standard Hindi
- **Mumbai:** Bambaiya Hindi, Marathi influence
- **UP (Lucknow/Kanpur):** Awadhi-influenced
- **Bihar:** Bhojpuri-influenced Hindi
- **South Indian-accented:** Tamil/Telugu/Kannada speakers' Hindi
- **Rajasthani-accented:** Marwari influence
- **Punjabi-accented:** Punjab Hindi speakers

## 4. Spanish (`es`)
- **Castilian (Spain):** Madrid, distinction of c/z
- **Mexican:** Mexico City, Northern, Yucatan
- **Argentine:** Rioplatense (Buenos Aires), Cordobes
- **Colombian:** Bogota, Paisa (Medellin), Costeno
- **Caribbean:** Cuban, Puerto Rican, Dominican
- **Chilean:** Santiago, distinct intonation
- **Peruvian:** Lima, Andean-accented

## 5. Arabic (`ar`)
- **Egyptian (Masri):** Cairo, Sa'idi (Upper Egypt)
- **Gulf (Khaliji):** Saudi, Emirati, Kuwaiti
- **Levantine:** Syrian, Lebanese, Jordanian, Palestinian
- **Maghrebi:** Moroccan, Algerian, Tunisian
- **Iraqi:** Baghdadi
- **MSA (Modern Standard Arabic):** news/formal register
- **Sudanese:** Khartoum

## 6. French (`fr`)
- **Parisian/Standard:** Ile-de-France
- **Canadian Quebecois:** Montreal, Quebec City
- **West African:** Senegalese, Ivorian, Cameroonian
- **Belgian:** Brussels, Walloon
- **Swiss French:** Geneva
- **Southern France:** Marseille, Toulouse (Occitan influence)
- **North African:** Algerian-accented, Moroccan-accented

## 7. Bengali (`bn`)
- **Kolkata (West Bengal):** standard Bangla
- **Dhaka (Bangladesh):** standard Bangladeshi
- **Sylheti-accented:** northeastern Bangladesh
- **Chittagong:** southeastern Bangladesh
- **Medinipur/Rarhi:** western West Bengal
- **Rangpuri:** northern Bangladesh

## 8. Portuguese (`pt`)
- **Brazilian - Sao Paulo:** paulistano
- **Brazilian - Rio:** carioca
- **Brazilian - Northeast:** Bahia, Pernambuco
- **Brazilian - South:** gaucho (RS), parana
- **European (Lisbon):** standard continental
- **European (Porto):** northern Portugal
- **Angolan Portuguese:** Luanda
- **Mozambican Portuguese**

## 9. Russian (`ru`)
- **Moscow:** standard literary
- **St. Petersburg:** slight lexical differences
- **Siberian:** Novosibirsk, eastern regions
- **Southern (Kuban):** Ukrainian-influenced
- **Ural:** Yekaterinburg region
- **Central Asian-accented:** Kazakh/Uzbek speakers' Russian

## 10. Japanese (`ja`)
- **Tokyo/Standard (Hyojungo):** NHK-style
- **Kansai (Osaka/Kyoto):** distinct pitch accent
- **Tohoku:** northern, softer consonants
- **Kyushu (Hakata):** southwestern
- **Hokkaido:** minor differences
- **Elderly formal speech vs. young casual speech**

## 11. German (`de`)
- **Hochdeutsch (Hannover):** standard
- **Bavarian-accented:** Munich, southern
- **Austrian:** Vienna, Graz
- **Swiss German-accented:** Zurich, Bern
- **Saxon:** Dresden, Leipzig
- **Swabian:** Stuttgart region
- **Berlin dialect-influenced**

## 12. Korean (`ko`)
- **Seoul (Pyojuneo):** standard
- **Busan/Gyeongsang:** southeastern, strong intonation
- **Jeolla:** southwestern
- **Chungcheong:** central, slow tempo
- **Gangwon:** northeastern
- **Jeju:** highly distinctive (older speakers)

## 13. Tamil (`ta`)
- **Chennai:** urban educated
- **Madurai:** central Tamil Nadu, heavier
- **Kanyakumari:** southern tip, Nagercoil
- **Coimbatore/Kongu:** western TN
- **Sri Lankan Tamil:** Jaffna, Colombo
- **Tirunelveli:** deep south
- **Thanjavur/Trichy:** central-eastern

## 14. Turkish (`tr`)
- **Istanbul (standard):** educated urban
- **Anatolian (various):** Kayseri, Konya
- **Black Sea (Karadeniz):** Trabzon, rapid speech
- **Southeastern:** Diyarbakir, Kurdish-influenced
- **Aegean (Izmir):** western
- **Elderly rural vs. young urban**

## 15. Vietnamese (`vi`)
- **Northern (Hanoi):** standard
- **Central (Hue):** distinct tones and vocabulary
- **Southern (Saigon/Ho Chi Minh):** merged tones, softer
- **Nghe An/Ha Tinh:** north-central, heavy accent
- **Mekong Delta:** far southern

## 16. Italian (`it`)
- **Standard (Florence/Rome):** RAI Italian
- **Neapolitan-accented:** Naples, Campania
- **Sicilian-accented:** Palermo, Catania
- **Northern (Milan):** Lombard influence
- **Venetian-accented:** Veneto region
- **Sardinian-accented:** Sardinia
- **Tuscan:** strong gorgia

## 17. Thai (`th`)
- **Central (Bangkok):** standard
- **Northern (Chiang Mai/Lanna):** Kam Muang influence
- **Isan (Northeastern):** Lao-influenced
- **Southern:** Nakhon Si Thammarat, Hat Yai
- **Formal vs. casual register**

## 18. Polish (`pl`)
- **Warsaw (standard):** educated urban
- **Silesian-accented:** Katowice region
- **Krakow (Lesser Poland):** southern
- **Poznan (Greater Poland):** western
- **Podlasie:** eastern, Belarusian influence
- **Kashubian-accented:** Gdansk/Pomerania

## 19. Dutch (`nl`)
- **Randstad (Amsterdam/The Hague):** ABN standard
- **Flemish Belgian:** Brussels, Antwerp, Ghent
- **Brabant:** southern Netherlands
- **Limburg:** southeastern, tonal
- **Frisian-accented:** northern Netherlands
- **Surinamese Dutch:** South American variety

## 20. Ukrainian (`uk`)
- **Kyiv:** standard central
- **Lviv (Western):** Polish influence, surzhyk-free
- **Odesa (Southern):** Russian influence, distinct
- **Kharkiv (Eastern):** surzhyk-influenced
- **Poltava/Central:** "purest" standard claim
- **Zakarpattia:** Hungarian/Slovak influence

## 21. Malay/Indonesian (`ms`/`id`)
- **Malaysian Standard (KL):** formal Malay
- **Javanese-accented Indonesian:** Central/East Java
- **Sundanese-accented:** West Java
- **Balinese-accented:** Bali
- **Sumatran (Medan):** Batak-influenced
- **Sabah/Sarawak Malay:** East Malaysian
- **Betawi (Jakarta):** informal Jakarta slang

## 22. Swahili (`sw`)
- **Kenyan (Nairobi):** urban, English-influenced
- **Tanzanian (Dar es Salaam):** considered "purer" Swahili
- **Coastal (Mombasa/Zanzibar):** original variety
- **Congolese (Lubumbashi):** DRC variant
- **Ugandan Swahili**

## 23. Telugu (`te`)
- **Telangana:** Hyderabad, Urdu-influenced lexicon
- **Coastal Andhra:** Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada
- **Rayalaseema:** Kurnool, Anantapur, heavier
- **Godavari districts:** East/West Godavari
- **Urban Hyderabad youth vs. rural elderly**

## 24. Urdu (`ur`)
- **Lahore (Punjab):** Punjabi-influenced
- **Karachi (Sindh):** Muhajir standard
- **Islamabad:** formal, government register
- **Peshawar:** Pashto-influenced
- **Indian Urdu (Lucknow):** refined Lakhnavi
- **Indian Urdu (Hyderabad Deccan):** Dakhni

## 25. Tagalog/Filipino (`tl`)
- **Metro Manila:** standard Filipino
- **Visayan-accented:** Cebuano speakers' Tagalog
- **Ilocano-accented:** Northern Luzon
- **Bicolano-accented:** Southern Luzon
- **Mindanao:** Davao, Muslim Mindanao influence
- **Overseas Filipino:** code-switching heavy

## 26. Persian/Farsi (`fa`)
- **Tehrani:** standard, urban
- **Isfahani:** central, distinct intonation
- **Mashhadi:** northeastern (Khorasani)
- **Shirazi:** southern
- **Afghan Dari (Kabul):** Afghan variant
- **Tajik-accented:** Central Asian Persian

## 27. Romanian (`ro`)
- **Bucharest (Muntenian):** standard
- **Moldovan:** Republic of Moldova, Russian influence
- **Transylvanian:** Cluj, Hungarian influence
- **Banat:** Timisoara, western
- **Oltenian:** Craiova, southwestern

## 28. Czech (`cs`)
- **Prague (standard):** Bohemian
- **Moravian:** Brno, Olomouc, softer
- **Silesian:** Ostrava, Polish influence
- **Hanak (Central Moravian):** distinctive vowels

## 29. Greek (`el`)
- **Athenian:** standard modern Greek
- **Cretan:** southern island, distinct
- **Cypriot:** Cyprus, different phonology
- **Thessaloniki (Macedonian):** northern
- **Pontic-accented:** Black Sea diaspora influence
- **Island varieties:** Dodecanese, Cycladic

## 30. Amharic (`am`)
- **Addis Ababa:** standard, urban
- **Gondar (Amhara):** northern
- **Wollo:** northeastern
- **Gojjam:** central highlands
- **Shewa:** central, historically prestigious
- **Diaspora (diaspora youth):** English-influenced
